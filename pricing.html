Questions

Is this live today?

The open environment and live demo are available now. Hosted runs and the Teams features are in early access and rolling out to the first users.

What will paid plans cost?

We haven't set final prices. Early-access teams get the beta free and a say in how pricing is shaped.

Can I bring my own model?

Yes. The environment follows the OpenEnv step, reset and state interface, so any agent that can emit the JSON actions can run against it.
